BURHAN

Verifiable answers from the Qur'an and authenticated Sunni hadith.

Ask a question about Islam and Burhan shows you the verse itself, word for word, with its reference and a link to the original. No AI writes the answers, so it can't make up a verse the way a chatbot can. It runs on your phone with no internet connection. And if it doesn't have a sound source for what you asked, it tells you that instead of guessing.

Most "AI Islam" apps can make things up

[ THIS ONE CAN'T ]

A chatbot writes its answers. That means it can produce a verse or a hadith that never existed, in language that sounds completely authoritative, and people then share it. Burhan doesn't write anything at all. It finds the passage you asked about and puts the stored text on screen with its reference attached. There is no text-generating model inside the app that could go wrong.

Word for word Every answer is the text exactly as it was given to the app. Nothing is paraphrased or summarised.

Your searches stay on your phone There is no account and no server. Searching never touches the network, so what you ask about your own religion stays yours.

How it works

[ ASK · IT SEARCHES · YOU SEE THE SOURCE ]

You don't need the surah number or the right English term. Ask in plain language, or in Arabic, and it finds the passage by meaning.

01
ASK IN YOUR OWN WORDS "Is interest allowed?" · "what does the quran say about parents" · a verse typed out in Arabic. It knows everyday religious vocabulary too, so asking about zakat finds the verses even though the translation calls it alms.
02
YOUR PHONE DOES THE SEARCHING It compares your question against all 6,236 verses, in English and Arabic, right on the device. No search request is sent anywhere. That is why it works offline, and why nobody (including me) can see what you searched for.
03
YOU GET THE PASSAGE AND ITS SOURCE The translation, the Arabic, the surah and verse, and a link to the original. Or "I don't have a sound source for that", which counts as a real answer here.

It would rather say nothing

[ WHAT IT DOES WHEN IT DOESN'T KNOW ]

An app about scripture that guesses is worse than no app at all. So Burhan is built to refuse. If it isn't confident that it has a real source for your question, it says so and shows you nothing.

It also never pretends its scope is bigger than it is. Every answer and every refusal carries the same line about what it can and cannot check. You never have to wonder whether silence means there was nothing to find, or that the app wasn't looking there.

What's inside today

[ NO SURPRISES ]

Being straight about scope is the whole point of the app, so it would be strange to get vague about it here.

In the app now
  • The complete Qur'an: all 114 surahs and all 6,236 verses.
  • Uthmani Arabic from the Tanzil Project, stored exactly as published.
  • The Clear Quran® by Dr. Mustafa Khattab, used with written permission, including all 1,335 footnotes.
  • Search in English and Arabic, including verses typed out word for word.
  • Everything offline, inside the app download. Nothing to stream or top up.
Not yet
  • Hadith. The collections aren't bundled yet, and the app says so on every answer instead of leaving you to assume.
  • Grades and graders. When hadith does land, every one will carry its authenticity grade and the name of the scholar who gave it.
  • An Arabic interface. Drafted, but held back until a qualified Arabic speaker has reviewed it properly.
  • Android. It builds and runs, it just isn't published yet.

The scope is Sunni, and the app says so. Burhan shows you sources. It doesn't issue rulings or fatwas, and it takes no position between schools of thought. For anything that needs a ruling, ask a qualified scholar. This is a tool for finding what the text actually says.

Questions

[ THE ONES I GET ASKED ]
Can it invent a verse, like ChatGPT sometimes does?

No, and not because it was told not to. There is no text-generating model inside the app. It can only find and display text that is already stored on your phone, so there is nothing in it capable of making something up.

Does it need internet?

Never. The whole Qur'an and the search itself ship inside the app. The only time it touches the network is if you tap a citation link to open the original source in your browser.

What do you do with my searches?

Nothing, because they never reach me. There's no account and no server, and searching never touches the network. There is one exception and it's entirely your choice: while the app is in testing, a bad result has a "report this" button. Only if you tap it does anything get sent, and what goes is your search plus which verses came back, named by reference. No Qur'an text, nothing that identifies you, and the app shows you the whole thing before it sends it.

Which translation is it?

The Clear Quran® by Dr. Mustafa Khattab, bundled with written permission from the publisher. The Arabic is the Uthmani text from the Tanzil Project. Both are stored exactly as published and never edited.

Why isn't hadith in there yet?

Doing it properly means every hadith carries a grade from a named scholar, and the data needed for that hasn't been made available to me yet. Shipping without it would mean showing gradings that nobody actually gave, which is the problem this app exists to avoid.

Will it give me a ruling?

No. It shows you what the sources say and where to find them. It isn't a replacement for a scholar.
