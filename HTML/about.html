Bio

[ The short version ]

I'm Layth, known online as layth49. I'm a software developer and automation engineer, which mostly means I look at anything repetitive and ask whether a script could be doing it instead. That started with small aim/typing/reaction bots, then grew into full assistants and apps that other people actually use.

My flagship project is L.A.I.T.H.49, a voice assistant for Windows that routes what I say to one of 38 tools through an LLM. I also build Burhan, a free, fully offline iOS app (currently on TestFlight) that answers questions about Islam with the Qur'an verse itself, always cited, with no AI writing the answers. Alongside that: Unity games, trading and scraping bots, and a growing pile of hardware experiments with Arduino and Raspberry Pi.

I work primarily in Python, C#, and Swift, and I'm based in the U.S.A. If it should be automated, scripted, or have an AI shoved inside it, that's usually where I end up spending my time.
