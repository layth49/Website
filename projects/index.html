A free, fully offline app that answers questions about Islam with the verse itself, word for word, with its reference and a link to the original. No AI writes the answers, so it can't invent a verse the way a chatbot can. All 6,236 verses ship inside the download. Hadith isn't bundled yet, and the app says so on every answer.
